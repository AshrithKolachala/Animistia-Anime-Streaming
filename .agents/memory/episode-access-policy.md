---
name: Animistia episode access
description: The product rule for guest and signed-in episode playback.
---

Guests can watch Season 1, Episode 1 without signing in. Every other episode requires a signed-in account, and all episodes remain free after sign-in; there is no subscription requirement.

**Why:** The user requested a free first episode for visitors and free access to all episodes after login.

**How to apply:** Keep this policy consistent across the watch page and media API. Treat Season 1, Episode 1 as the free preview.
